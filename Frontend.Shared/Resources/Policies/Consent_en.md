## 5. Duration of consent and the procedure for withdrawing it

5.1. This Consent is valid from the moment it is given until the processing purposes are achieved, but not longer than the retention periods established in the Personal Data Processing Policy.

5.2. Consent may be withdrawn at any time. A withdrawal is sent in writing to the Operator using the details in the Personal Data Processing Policy, or by deleting the account in the Service interface.

5.3. After withdrawal the Operator ceases processing and destroys the data within 10 working days from receipt of the withdrawal, unless otherwise provided by federal law and unless processing may continue on other grounds.

Consent in written form is given by ticking the "I agree to the processing of personal data" box on the Site's registration page, and by ticking the same box when consent is reconfirmed in the Service interface. The box is ticked after reviewing this Consent and the Personal Data Processing Policy.
